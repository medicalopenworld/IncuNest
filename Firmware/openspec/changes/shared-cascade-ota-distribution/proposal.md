## Why

Today each board updates itself, and two of the three cannot be updated in the field at all. `Display_HMI` provisions itself as a **separate ThingsBoard device** (`IncuNest-Display-<sn>`, own token in NVS, own `PROVISION_DEVICE_KEY`) and pulls its own package with `Start_Firmware_Update` under the title `IncuNest_HMI` (`Display_HMI/src/tasks/Wifi_OTA.cpp:336-341`, `:343-416`). `SensorBoard_v2` has **no remote update path whatsoever** — not a single `esp_ota_*` call exists in its code, even though its partition table now provides two real OTA slots plus `otadata` (`SensorBoard_v2/partitions.csv`, revised 2026-09-06). And because the HMI has no GPRS modem and the SensorBoard has no radio at all, **a unit deployed on GPRS only can never update either of them**: the incubator is permanently frozen on whatever those two boards were flashed with at the factory.

This change makes `motherBoard` the only device registered in ThingsBoard and the only updater in the system: it fetches a versioned three-image bundle and pushes the images to `Display_HMI` over the existing UART link and to `SensorBoard_v2` over the existing USB CDC link.

Boards affected: **motherBoard** (bundle fetch, orchestration, both transfer paths, cloud identity), **Display_HMI** (OTA receiver over UART, removal of its ThingsBoard client, diagnostics relayed to the motherBoard), and **shared/** (the bundle-manifest and transfer-status types both boards must agree on). The `SensorBoard_v2` receiver side lives in that sub-project's own OpenSpec root (`SensorBoard_v2/openspec/`, change `sb-ota-receiver`) and is a hard dependency of this one — never merged into this change per this root's scope rule.

A second motive is protocol coupling. `Firmware/PROTOCOL.md` records that v2.0.0, v2.1.0 and v2.2.0 each broke the motherBoard↔HMI parse and that "ambas placas deben actualizarse juntas". With two independent ThingsBoard devices, nothing enforces that: an operator can assign a package to one and not the other, and the incubator runs mismatched until someone notices. A single device carrying a single **version set** makes the coupling structural instead of procedural.

This change touches three areas with documented history in `Firmware/docs/known_issues.md` and must not reintroduce any of them:

- **Issue 2 (cyclic UART flooding)**: the link already collapsed once under a high-rate `CTRL,TEL` burst saturating the HMI's LVGL/DMA path. Streaming megabytes down that same wire is the same failure mode with a bigger payload — hence the mandatory quiesced telemetry and ACK-gated flow control below.
- **Issue 5 (CH340 bootload pile-up)**: spurious levels on TX/RX and RTS/DTR left the motherboard "catatonic in Waiting Download Mode". This change deliberately does **not** attempt to drive any board into ROM download mode; see Non-goals.
- **Issue 6 (open, AUDIO PAUSED indicator)**: its investigation notes record that the HMI's only UART *is* the protocol link, so HMI-side `COMM_LOG` output travels down the same wire the motherBoard parses. Every frame of this transfer protocol therefore has to survive arbitrary interleaved log and ROM-boot text, exactly as the existing crash-capture path in `motherBoard/src/tasks/CommTask.cpp:413-417` already does.

## What Changes

- **ThingsBoard carries a manifest, not images.** A ThingsBoard device exposes exactly one `fw_*` slot (plus one `sw_*`), so three images cannot travel through the native mechanism on a single device. The package assigned to the motherboard becomes a small JSON **bundle manifest** (set version, and per board: version, URL, size, SHA-256) under a new firmware title, fetched through the already-proven `Firmware_Send_Info`/`Start_Firmware_Update` path on both transports. The three `.bin` files are then fetched by HTTPS with `Range` requests, so a dropped link resumes instead of restarting. The dashboard workflow documented in `Firmware/docs/thingsboard_dashboards.md` §8 (assign package → device downloads) is unchanged.
- **New motherBoard OTA orchestrator**: a maintenance-gated state machine that installs the whole set or none of it — SensorBoard first, then HMI, then the motherboard's own image, each written to its inactive slot with no boot switch, followed by a coordinated commit-and-reset of all three.
- **New `FWUP_*` message set on the motherBoard↔HMI UART** (`Firmware/PROTOCOL.md` minor version bump): offer/accept, base64 chunks sized to fit the HMI's existing 1280-byte `rxBuffer` (`Display_HMI/include/main.h:210`), one outstanding chunk at a time, per-chunk sequence and CRC, terminal SHA-256 verification, commit and abort. Telemetry is quiesced for the duration; alarm traffic (`CTRL,ALM`, `CTRL,STATE`) is never suppressed.
- **New motherBoard-side SensorBoard OTA frames** over the existing `usb_comm` binary framing (Magic + Type + Len + CRC16-CCITT), reusing `modules/sensorboard_comm/`, which stays the single owner of the device handle.
- **New maintenance gate**: the orchestrator refuses to start unless AIR and SKIN control are off, no baby profile is active, heater and humidifier are idle, and the unit is on mains. Rationale is clinical, not cosmetic: the SensorBoard's SHT40s *are* the air/humidity variable of the PID and the source of the thermal cut-offs (`motherBoard/src/modules/sensorboard_comm/sensorboard_comm.h`), and a rebooting HMI is an incubator with no visual alarm indication.
- **New rollback arbitration**: the motherboard is the judge of whether a slave's new image is good — the HMI must send `HMI,UI_READY` and the SensorBoard a heartbeat within a bounded window, otherwise the previous image is restored. Both slaves keep two OTA slots, so a power cut mid-write leaves the old image bootable.
- **BREAKING (cloud, not wire): `Display_HMI` stops being a ThingsBoard device.** Its provisioning, token, TB client, `WIFI_TB_OTA()`, `WIFICheckOTA()` and `CURRENT_FIRMWARE_TITLE "IncuNest_HMI"` are removed, and `PROVISION_DEVICE_KEY`/`PROVISION_DEVICE_SECRET` disappear from the HMI build. Existing `IncuNest-Display-<sn>` devices in ThingsBoard become orphaned and any dashboard widget bound to them must be re-pointed; this is a one-way migration.
- **New `HMI,DIAG` message** so nothing is lost by that removal: the seven diagnostic keys the HMI publishes today (`hmi_heap_int_b`, `hmi_heap_int_min_b`, `hmi_heap_psram_b`, `hmi_stack_ui_b`, `hmi_stack_comm_b`, plus `fw_version`/`sn`) travel to the motherboard and are republished under the motherboard's device with the **same key names**, so most widgets survive a device re-point.
- **New motherBoard attributes `hmi_fw_version` and `sb_fw_version`** plus a per-target dimension on the existing `ota_progress` telemetry (`motherBoard/include/config/telemetry_keys.h:149`), so the fleet table shows which board is at what percentage and what each board is running. ThingsBoard's native *FW state: synced* column keeps tracking only the set version reported by the motherboard.
- **New `installBundle` RPC**, registered in **both** transport lists. `Firmware/docs/thingsboard_dashboards.md` records that `checkOta` was once registered only on the GPRS side and silently did not exist over WiFi; the two `RPC_Callback` arrays (`motherBoard/src/tasks/GPRS.cpp:190`, `motherBoard/src/tasks/Wifi_OTA.cpp:1221`) are independent and both must be updated.
- **The HMI keeps its WiFi STA, mDNS and HTTP `Update.h` endpoint** as the recovery path, unchanged. `flasher_tool/flasher/wifi_flasher.py` depends on it for the factory floor, and once the motherboard is the only updater it is also the only way back from a bad HMI image.
- **The SensorBoard image joins the release artifacts**: `flasher_tool/flasher/updater.py` `_ASSET_MAP` publishes motherboard and HMI assets from GitHub Releases but has no SensorBoard entry, even though `flasher_tool/data/firmware/sensorboard/` exists. The bundle manifest cannot reference an artifact that CI never publishes.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `ota-bundle-manifest`: the bundle-manifest format and its ThingsBoard contract — what the assigned package contains, the version-set semantics, how the motherboard fetches and validates it (schema, size ceiling, SHA-256 per image), and how it decides that a set is already installed. Kept separate from the orchestrator because it is a data contract shared with the release/CI side, versioned independently of the install state machine.
- `ota-cascade-orchestrator`: the motherBoard state machine — maintenance preconditions and refusal reasons, install order, two-phase write-then-commit, resumption after a dropped network link or a reboot mid-bundle, rollback arbitration for both slaves, progress and outcome reporting, and the mismatched-version recovery path when a power cut splits a commit.
- `ota-hmi-transfer-protocol`: the `FWUP_*` message set over the motherBoard↔HMI UART and the HMI-side receiver that writes to the inactive slot. Covers framing under interleaved log/ROM text, chunk sizing against the existing buffers, ACK-gated flow control, telemetry quiescing with alarms exempt, error codes, and the `PROTOCOL.md` update. Separate capability because it is a wire contract that must stay verifiable on its own, and because the HMI receiver is the one piece with no automated test environment.
- `ota-sensorboard-transfer`: the motherBoard half of the SensorBoard transfer — new `usb_comm` frame types, chunking over the CDC link, the single-owner rule for the device handle, and the sensor-fault/PID interaction while the SensorBoard is rebooting into its new image. The SensorBoard-side receiver is specified in `SensorBoard_v2/openspec/` and is out of scope here.
- `single-cloud-device-identity`: the motherboard as the only cloud identity — removal of the HMI's provisioning, token and TB client, the `HMI,DIAG` relay that preserves the `hmi_*` telemetry keys, the `hmi_fw_version`/`sb_fw_version` attributes, and the dashboard migration for the orphaned `IncuNest-Display-<sn>` devices.

### Modified Capabilities
(none — the three existing specs in `openspec/specs/` are `hmi-help-center`, `hmi-link-loss-audio` and `hmi-maintenance-reminder`, and none of their requirements change. The HMI's ThingsBoard client and the boards' self-OTA paths were never formalized as OpenSpec capabilities.)

## Impact

- **Affected code — shared/**: new manifest and transfer-status types (`shared/include/ota_bundle.h`, and the `FWUP_*` field layouts both boards parse), consumed via `lib_extra_dirs = ../shared`. If a `.cpp` is added, `motherBoard/platformio.ini`'s `[env:native]` `build_src_filter` must be extended in the same commit and covered by Unity tests, per `.claude/rules/testing.md`.
- **Affected code — motherBoard**: new orchestrator module (likely `src/modules/ota/`), `src/tasks/Wifi_OTA.cpp` and `src/tasks/GPRS.cpp` (manifest path alongside the existing self-OTA, new `installBundle` RPC in both `RPC_Callback` arrays, `ota_progress` gains a target dimension, new `hmi_fw_version`/`sb_fw_version` attributes and the relayed `hmi_*` keys), `src/tasks/CommTask.cpp` (`FWUP_*` TX/RX beside the existing line parser and the HMI crash-capture path), `src/modules/sensorboard_comm/` (OTA frames), `include/config/telemetry_keys.h`.
- **Affected code — Display_HMI**: `src/tasks/Wifi_OTA.cpp` (TB client, provisioning and `WIFI_TB_OTA()`/`WIFICheckOTA()` removed; WiFi STA, mDNS and the HTTP `Update.h` endpoint kept), `src/tasks/CommTask.cpp` (OTA receiver over UART, `HMI,DIAG` sender), `include/Credentials.h` (TB provisioning keys removed), plus a maintenance/updating screen that holds the UI while the transfer runs.
- **Affected docs**: `Firmware/PROTOCOL.md` (new `FWUP_*` and `HMI,DIAG` messages, minor version bump), `Firmware/docs/communication.md`, `Firmware/docs/thingsboard_dashboards.md` (single-device model, the migration, the new RPC), `Firmware/docs/transport_matrix.md` (bundle-check cadence and which images each transport may deliver).
- **Affected tooling**: `flasher_tool/flasher/updater.py` `_ASSET_MAP` and the release CI gain the SensorBoard assets; the bundle-manifest artifact itself has to be produced by the release process.
- **Hard dependency**: `SensorBoard_v2/openspec/` change `sb-ota-receiver` (its first `esp_ota_*` code path). The orchestrator cannot be verified end to end before that lands, though the motherBoard and HMI halves can.
- **Depends on a pending hardware measurement**: `motherBoard/platformio.ini` declares no `board_build.flash_size` and inherits 8 MB, giving a 2.625 MB LittleFS that already holds baby profiles and weight history — so this change specifies **streaming**, never staging an image locally. If `esptool.py flash_id` confirms a 16 MB module (`motherBoard/partitions/ESP32S3_16MB.csv` is ready, filesystem 7.875 MB), a store-and-forward variant that verifies SHA-256 *before* touching a slave becomes possible; see Non-goals.
- **Testing**: Unity tests in motherBoard's `[env:native]` are mandatory for the pure logic — manifest parsing and validation (including malformed, truncated and oversized manifests), version-set comparison, install-order and precondition decision logic, chunk sequencing/CRC and the `FWUP_*` field-count/numeric validation, and base64 encode/decode boundaries. Everything with hardware or a radio in the loop is **manual verification only** and must be documented as such: the real HTTPS/Range fetch, the UART transfer against a real HMI, the CDC transfer against a real SensorBoard, both rollback windows, the power-cut-mid-commit recovery, and the dashboard migration.
- **Out of scope (Non-goals)**:
  - Driving any board into ROM download mode / an esptool-style flow from the motherboard. There is no DTR/RTS wired to the HMI's EN/IO0 and no reset GPIO in `motherBoard/include/config/board.h`, and `known_issues.md` issue 5 is exactly what that class of attempt cost last time.
  - Baud escalation on the HMI link during transfer. 2.53 MB base64-encoded at 115200 is roughly five minutes, which a maintenance screen absorbs; raising the baud rate is a measured optimization for later, not a v1 requirement.
  - Delivering the HMI image over GPRS. The manifest declares a per-image minimum transport, and 2.53 MB over GPRS is not realistic; the SensorBoard's ~356 KB is, and is allowed.
  - Store-and-forward staging in LittleFS (blocked on the flash-size measurement above).
  - Removing the HMI's WiFi stack entirely. It would free flash that the pending Amharic font work needs, but it is also the recovery path and the factory tool's transport — a separate decision.
  - Cryptographic **signature** verification of images. This change relies on SHA-256 from a manifest delivered over TLS by ThingsBoard, which gives integrity and transport-level authenticity but not image signing; secure boot and signed images are a follow-up proposal.
