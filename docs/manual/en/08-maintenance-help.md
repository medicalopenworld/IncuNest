# 8. Maintenance, help and troubleshooting

## 8.1 Reminders

The incubator can remind you when cleaning, weighing or service is due. Switch each reminder on or off in **Settings > MAINTENANCE**:

| Switch | Reminds you to |
|---|---|
| **REMINDERS** | Clean the incubator (daily, weekly and terminal cleaning; see 7.4). |
| **WEIGHING REMINDER** | Weigh the baby every 24 hours (see 5.9). |
| **SERVICE REMINDER** | Have the heater, fan, humidifier and phototherapy lamp serviced after their hours of use. |

![Settings — maintenance](img/settings_maintenance_en.png)

When a reminder is due, it appears when you unlock the screen. You can also open it at any time with **OPEN REMINDER** in the same settings panel.

### Cleaning reminder

![Cleaning reminder](img/maintenance_cleaning_en.png)

The cleaning reminder shows the three cleaning levels. Each level shows **DUE NOW** or **up to date**, and the date it was last done.

- After cleaning, press **DONE** for that level. The incubator records it and counts the next period from now.
- If the reminder appeared by itself when you unlocked the screen, press **LATER** to be reminded again in 24 hours. [PENDING: check on the bench; opened with OPEN REMINDER, the view shows only SERVICE and CLOSE.]
- Press **CLOSE** to return without recording anything.
- Scan the QR code with a phone camera to watch the cleaning and maintenance tutorials.

### Service reminder

![Service reminder](img/maintenance_service_en.png)

The service view shows, for the heater, the fan, the humidifier and the phototherapy lamp, how many hours each one has worked since its last service, out of the hours allowed.

When a part reaches its hours, contact technical support to arrange the service. Only technical personnel record the service as done (see chapter 10).

## 8.2 Help on the touchscreen

Open **Menu > HELP**. The help window has three options:

![Help](img/help_menu_en.png)

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **GUIDED TUTORIAL** | Training courses on the touchscreen itself, for nursing staff and for technicians (see 8.3). |
| **VIDEO TUTORIAL** | A QR code to watch video tutorials on your phone. The web address is also shown, to type it by hand. |
| **CONTACT SUPPORT** | A QR code that opens an email to technical support on your phone, ready to send (see 8.5). |

The help window closes by itself after 3 minutes without use.

## 8.3 Training mode

Training mode teaches staff to use the incubator with short lessons on the touchscreen, using a practice baby called **ZOE**.

::: warning
**WARNING** In training mode the incubator really heats the chamber and switches the phototherapy lamp on. Use training mode only with the chamber empty.
:::

### Starting a lesson

1. Open **Menu > HELP > GUIDED TUTORIAL**.
2. Choose a course, type the student's name (at least 2 letters) and choose a lesson.
3. Confirm that the chamber is empty.

![Choosing a course](img/training_selector_en.png)

A band at the top of the screen shows that a lesson is running.

- If the incubator is free (no function switched on, no active alarm), the lesson runs for real: the band says **TRAINING: THE INCUBATOR REALLY HEATS AND LIGHTS UP. EMPTY CABIN.**
- Otherwise, the lesson runs as a **demonstration**, without heating or switching the lamp on.

![Lesson running](img/training_lesson_banner_en.png)

A lesson stops by itself if an alarm appears, if the screen loses contact with the control board, or after 3 minutes without use. When you leave a lesson, the incubator returns to how it was before.

### Courses

Both courses start with the same introductory lesson, **Meet the screen**.

| Course | Lessons |
|---|---|
| **Nursing** | Meet the screen · Registering and following a baby · Air temperature · Skin control and probe (only if skin mode is enabled) · Humidity (only if humidity control is enabled) · Safe phototherapy · Handling an alarm · Baby exit · Screen lock · Setting the time · Contacting support |
| **Technician** | See chapter 10 |

When a student completes all the lessons available, the incubator shows a **certificate** with a QR code. Progress is saved on the incubator.

![Certificate](img/training_certificate_en.png)

The list of registered babies is not available during training.

## 8.4 Troubleshooting

Problems that do not raise an alarm. For alarms, see chapter 6.

| Problem | Possible cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| The clock shows **No time**. | The date and time have not been set, or have been lost. | Set them in Menu > DATE AND TIME (see 4.4). |
| **SKIN MODE** cannot be switched on ("Skin mode unavailable"). | The skin temperature probe is not connected. | Connect the probe, then switch skin mode on. |
| The TEMPERATURE block has disappeared from the main screen. | A **FAN FAILURE** or **HEATER FAULT** alarm is active. | See chapter 6. |
| The SKIN panel does not appear on the main screen. | Skin mode is not enabled, or the probe is not connected. | Enable skin mode in Settings > MODES and connect the probe. |
| The HUMIDITY block does not appear. | Humidity control is disabled in the settings. | Enable **HUMIDITY CONTROL** in Settings > MODES. |
| The baby data wizard shows no proposed temperature. | No weight was entered, the gestational age is under 24 weeks, or the table has no value for that baby. | Set the air temperature by hand (see 5.3). |
| The wizard shows "No response from board" or "Operation refused, try again". | The screen did not get an answer from the control board. | Wait a few seconds and try again. If it continues, switch the incubator off and on. |
| "3 babies already active: discharge one first". | The incubator keeps up to 3 active babies. | Discharge a baby who has left (see 5.10). |
| SpO2, PR and the pulse wave do not appear on the lock screen. | The probe is not connected or not correctly applied, or there is too much light on it. | Check the probe, its position and its connection. |
| The measured values disappear and **LINK LOST** flashes. | The screen has lost contact with the control board. | Check the infant. Switch the incubator off and on. |
| The alarm test is refused ("An alarm is active"). | The test cannot run while an alarm is active. | Deal with the alarm first, then repeat the test. |
| The touchscreen does not respond. | [PENDING: check on the bench.] | Switch the incubator off and on. If it continues, contact technical support. |

## 8.5 Technical support and service

Installation, repairs and servicing must be done only by authorised, qualified technical personnel.

To contact technical support:

- Use **Menu > HELP > CONTACT SUPPORT** and scan the QR code with your phone. An email to technical support opens, with the incubator's serial number in the subject.
    - By default, the email also contains the incubator's technical status. Type your question above it and send it.
    - If you press **NO REPORT** before scanning, the email has only the recipient and the subject. Describe the problem in the email.
    - Press **BACK** to return to the help window.
- Or write directly to technical support. [PENDING: the touchscreen uses support@medicalopenworld.org, the previous manual gave contact@medicalopenworld.org. Confirm the address.]

![Contact support](img/help_contact_qr_en.png)

Have this information ready, from **Settings > INFO**: serial number (S/N), display version and motherboard version.
