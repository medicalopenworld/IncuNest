# 7. Cleaning and disinfection

Clean and disinfect the incubator as described in this chapter. Regular cleaning protects the infant from infection and extends the service life of the incubator.

## 7.1 Before you clean

::: warning
**WARNING** Switch the incubator off and disconnect it from the power supply before any cleaning.
:::

::: warning
**WARNING** Do not clean the inside of the chamber while an infant is in it. Move the infant safely to another place first. [PENDING: what the "daily, with a patient inside" level covers; see 7.4.]
:::

::: caution
**CAUTION** Do not use steam. Do not spray liquids directly onto the touchscreen or the connector panel.
:::

## 7.2 What you need

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| 70 % alcohol, or an equivalent medical-grade disinfectant | Disinfecting the mattress, the mattress tray, the skin temperature probe and the touchscreen |
| Mild, neutral, low-foaming detergent | Cleaning interior and exterior surfaces |
| Soft, non-abrasive, lint-free cloths | All surfaces |
| Pre-moistened 70 % alcohol wipes | Skin temperature probe |

::: warning
**WARNING — Flammable disinfectants.** After using a flammable disinfectant such as alcohol, ventilate the chamber fully and let the disinfectant evaporate completely before you switch the heater on or connect the oxygen supply.
:::

::: note
**NOTE — Bleach (sodium hypochlorite).** If you use household bleach with about 5 % sodium hypochlorite, dilute 20 mL of bleach in 1 litre of water. For a different concentration, adjust the dilution in proportion. Never use concentrated or undiluted bleach.
:::

## 7.3 Cleaning procedure

1. Switch the incubator off, disconnect it from the power supply and move all cables away from the cleaning area.
2. Remove the mattress. Wipe it with a dry, lint-free cloth, then disinfect it with 70 % alcohol where suitable.
3. Remove the mattress tray from the chamber.
4. Clean all interior and exterior surfaces with a soft cloth lightly moistened with a mild neutral detergent.
5. Clean the exterior panels with a low-foaming detergent solution.
6. Disinfect the mattress tray with 70 % alcohol before you put it back in the chamber.
7. Wipe the skin temperature probe with a pre-moistened 70 % alcohol wipe.
8. Clean the pulse oximetry probe as described in its own instructions for use. [PENDING: which probe is supplied and its cleaning instructions.]
9. Empty and clean the humidifier water tank. [PENDING: depends on whether the humidifier is covered by this manual, and on its cleaning procedure.]
10. Wipe the touchscreen with a soft cloth lightly moistened with disinfectant.
11. Reassemble in reverse order: mattress tray, mattress, then the probe and power cables.
12. Make sure everything is completely dry before you switch the incubator on again.

The incubator's help screen and cleaning reminder show a QR code that links to video tutorials. The videos are optional: this manual contains all the instructions you need. [PENDING: whether the video tutorials are kept and updated for each version.]

## 7.4 How often to clean

The incubator reminds you when cleaning is due. The reminder appears when you unlock the screen, if cleaning reminders are switched on (see 8.1).

| Level | When | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Daily** | Every day while an infant is in the incubator | [PENDING: which surfaces can be cleaned with the infant inside.] |
| **Weekly** | Every 7 days, or sooner if visibly dirty | [PENDING: content of the weekly cleaning.] |
| **Terminal** | When the infant leaves the incubator, or every 7 days during a long stay | The full procedure in 7.3. |

After cleaning, press **DONE** for that level in the cleaning reminder, so that the incubator counts the next period from now.
