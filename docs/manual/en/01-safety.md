# 1. Safety

Read this chapter before using the incubator. Keep these instructions close to the incubator and make sure that everyone who uses it has been trained on them.

Safety notices in this manual use three signal words:

| Signal word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **WARNING** | A hazard that can cause death or serious injury to the infant or the user. |
| **CAUTION** | A hazard that can cause minor injury or damage to the incubator. |
| **NOTE** | Information that helps you use the incubator correctly. |

## 1.1 Intended use

IncuNest is a neonatal incubator. It provides a warm, controlled environment for the thermal protection of newborn infants in resource-limited settings.

## 1.2 Prohibited uses

::: warning
**WARNING — Fire and explosion.** Do not use the incubator in the presence of flammable anaesthetics or explosive gas mixtures, or near flammable cleaning agents or fuels. Even small amounts of flammable agents left inside the incubator, such as ether or alcohol, can catch fire when oxygen is in use.
:::

::: warning
**WARNING — Sparks.** Do not place any equipment that can produce sparks inside the incubator. Oxygen increases the risk of fire.
:::

::: warning
**WARNING — Environment.** Do not expose the incubator to rain, frost or direct sunlight. Direct sunlight or other external heat sources can raise the temperature inside the chamber quickly to dangerous levels.
:::

::: warning
**WARNING — One infant only.** Place only one infant in the incubator at a time.
:::

## 1.3 Warnings and cautions

The warnings below are grouped by topic. The most important ones are repeated in the chapters where you perform each task.

### Electrical safety

::: warning
**WARNING** Do not use the incubator if it is damaged or if the power cable is damaged. If you notice signs of damage, such as a smell of burning, disconnect the incubator from the power supply immediately and contact technical support.
:::

- Keep the power adaptor and the power cable away from hot surfaces.
- Do not let the power cable get trapped or rub against sharp edges.
- Do not let the power cable hang loosely: it can cause someone to trip, or be damaged.
- Do not immerse cables or connectors in water.
- Make sure that any other equipment connected to the infant is electrically safe.

### Oxygen

::: warning
**WARNING** Always use an oxygen analyser when you give oxygen to the infant.
:::

- Give supplementary oxygen only through the plastic inlet on the front panel.
- Giving oxygen can increase the noise level inside the incubator.

### Temperature and environment

::: warning
**WARNING** Do not leave the infant unattended. Stay close to the incubator. At least every 10 minutes, check that the skin temperature probe is still attached to the infant and that the temperatures are within limits.
:::

::: warning
**WARNING** Keep the cover and the access ports closed while the incubator is controlling temperature.
:::

- Keep the room between 22 °C and 26 °C, and always below the temperature set on the incubator.
- Place the incubator away from windows and out of direct sunlight. [PENDING: clinical review. The previous manual said to cover the incubator with a towel or blanket if sunlight could not be avoided; that could block the view of the infant and the airflow.]

### Skin temperature probe

::: warning
**WARNING** Do not use the skin temperature probe as a rectal temperature probe.
:::

::: warning
**WARNING** In skin temperature mode, the incubator cannot tell the difference between a rise in core temperature with cold skin (fever) and a low core and skin temperature (hypothermia). Check the infant's core temperature regularly with a clinical thermometer. [PENDING: clinical confirmation.]
:::

### Phototherapy

::: warning
**WARNING** Protect the infant's eyes with opaque eye shields whenever phototherapy is on. The incubator asks you to confirm this every time you switch phototherapy on.
:::

- Do not apply creams, lotions or oils to the infant's skin before or during phototherapy.
- The infant should wear only a nappy during phototherapy, so that as much skin as possible is exposed.

### Pulse oximetry

::: warning
**WARNING** IncuNest is not a patient monitor. It displays oxygen saturation (SpO2), pulse rate (PR) and perfusion index (PI) on the lock screen, but it does not raise any alarm for them. If the infant needs SpO2 or pulse rate monitoring, use a separate monitor with alarms. [PENDING: Pablo to confirm that this is the intended design.]
:::

- The values and the waveform are shown only while the probe is correctly applied. If the probe comes off, they disappear from the screen without any message.

### Alarms

::: warning
**WARNING** Alarms do not replace observing the infant. Respond to every alarm and check the infant first.
:::

- You can silence an alarm for 10 minutes. The alarm stays on the screen while it is silenced and sounds again if the problem is still present after 10 minutes.

### Training mode

::: warning
**WARNING** In training mode, the incubator really heats the chamber and switches the phototherapy lamp on. Make sure the chamber is empty before you start a lesson.
:::

### Care of the equipment

::: caution
**CAUTION** Do not put the incubator or any of its parts in a dishwasher.
:::

## 1.4 Regulatory status

IncuNest has been designed with reference to the basic safety and essential performance requirements of IEC 60601-1 and IEC 60601-2-19 (particular requirements for infant incubators).
