# 2. Device description

## 2.1 Overview

IncuNest is a neonatal incubator. The infant lies on a mattress inside a closed chamber with a cover and access ports. The incubator heats and circulates the air in the chamber, and can also control the humidity, deliver phototherapy and show the infant's skin temperature and pulse oximetry values. You control it from the touchscreen on the front (see chapter 3).
